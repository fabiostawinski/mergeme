# mergeme — Specifications & Requirements

## Overview

This is a tool that merges my google photos videos, in a single video to be added to youtube.

## Requirements

1 - It uses a google photos album to download the videos
2 - It also may consider the initial date for downloading the videos
3 - It first download the files, group them in several folders, each with 1 GB (may be more, as some videos are bigger), then with those folders it will use them to merge all videos in a single one. The goal is to merge it as quick as possible, so the folder might be have the videos merged in parallel.
4 - Once each folder has its once merged video, it will go to next step to merge, sequencially each folder video to create the final big video. Also it may add a open source, royalty free song to it, not loud, the voice and natural noise should be still hearable.
5 - It must add in the first second, the video title.
6 - It also adds the month and year, like "January 2026", once the video added iniciates that month. It shows only the next text, for the next month, and it lasts 5 seconds.
