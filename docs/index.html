Open-source media tool

mergeme

Turn a Google Photos export into one chronological, YouTube-ready video, with dates, month cards, photos, clips, and a quiet soundtrack.

Download releases View source

Cross-platform

Prebuilt binaries are available for Linux, Intel and Apple Silicon macOS, and Windows.

Simple runtime

Install ffmpeg, ffprobe, and exiftool, then point mergeme at your export.
